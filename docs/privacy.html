Privacy Policy — Melow Tic Tac Toe

Developer: Melow Labs • Package: com.melowlabs.melow_tic_tac_toe • Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Melow Tic Tac Toe uses Google Firebase Authentication (Anonymous Sign-In) and Cloud Firestore to provide real-time multiplayer gameplay and social features. We collect only the minimum information required to operate the game:

  • Anonymous Account Identifier: A randomly generated Firebase Authentication UID (no email address, phone number, or password is required).
  • Player Profile: Your chosen display nickname, selected avatar or optional gallery profile photo, preferred emoji mark, shareable friend code, and privacy/leaderboard preferences.
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Multiplayer room state, win/loss/draw statistics, Melow Cup trophies, achievements, head-to-head rivalry records, and optional leaderboard rankings.
  • Messages & Shared Images: Temporary room chat messages (filtered after 24 hours), live emoji reactions/quick taunts, and 1-on-1 direct friend chat messages and images you choose to send.
  • Real-Time Voice Call Audio: Microphone audio is captured only during user-initiated voice calls between friends to transmit live audio between participants. Voice audio is never permanently stored, archived, or used for analytics or advertising.
  • Safety Reports: Block lists and abuse reports submitted by players to keep the community safe.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly when you start or answer a live voice call with a friend.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to alert you of incoming chat messages, friend challenges, and voice calls.
  • Photo Gallery / Camera: Accessed only when you explicitly choose to upload a profile picture or share an image in chat.

3. How We Use & Protect Your Data

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS and WebRTC DTLS-SRTP connections.
  • We do not sell your personal information, show third-party behavioral ads, or share user data with data brokers.
  • Data is processed solely using Google Firebase infrastructure (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore).

4. Children’s Privacy

Melow Tic Tac Toe includes free-form player chat and voice calling and is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your anonymous player profile, profile photo, match stats, trophies, and chat data at any time:

  • Find your Friend Code in the app under Profile.
  • Email us at minoshdemel@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Melow Tic Tac Toe Data" and include your Friend Code or display nickname.
  • All associated Firestore profile, trophy, stat, and friend records will be deleted within 7 days.

6. Contact Us

For privacy questions, support, or data deletion requests, contact Melow Labs at minoshdemel@gmail.com.
